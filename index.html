To My Baby Maa, Chandana 💌

Happy Birthday, Baby Maa. ❤️

I honestly don't know where to begin, because 4–5 years is not something that can be explained in a few paragraphs.

We started as two people who found each other, became friends, became a cute couple, and somewhere along the way you became so much more than just one person in my life.

You have been my friend when I needed someone to laugh with. You have been like my mother when I needed care. You have been my teacher when I needed someone to show me the right way. You have been my guide when I felt lost. And sometimes, you have simply been the person I needed without even knowing that I needed you.

We have laughed over the stupidest things, fought over the smallest things, annoyed each other, understood each other, and created memories that I know I will never be able to replace.

There were good days. There were difficult days. There were days when we probably didn't even understand each other properly.

But somehow, after all these years, when I look back at everything, I don't remember the fights first.
I remember you.

The girl who made me laugh. The girl who cared for me. The girl who corrected me. The girl who taught me things. The girl who treated me like a child sometimes. 😂❤️

And that's why I call you Baby Maa.

Because you have been a little bit of everything to me.

I don't know what the future will look like. I don't know where life will take either of us. But I do know one thing...

You will always be one of the most beautiful chapters of my life.

Thank you for every laugh. Thank you for every lesson. Thank you for every fight. Thank you for every moment you stayed. Thank you for being there in ways you probably don't even realise.

And if this little website makes you smile, or maybe even brings a tiny happy tear to your eyes...

Then I know I did what I wanted to do.

Happy Birthday, my Baby Maa. 🎂❤️

No matter what happens, never forget that there is someone out there who will always be grateful that Chandana existed in his story.

With all the love, memories and gratitude,

Your PUCCHU ❤️
