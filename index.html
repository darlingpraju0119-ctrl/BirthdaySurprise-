To My Baby Maa

Happy Birthday, Chandana. ❤️

I don't really know where to begin because there are some people in life who simply cannot be explained in a few words.

And you are one of those people for me.

It's been around 4–5 years since you became such an important part of my life. We started as two people who became close, and somewhere along the way, you became so much more.

You became my friend when I needed someone to talk to.

You became my mother when I needed someone to care for me, scold me and keep me on the right path.

You became my teacher when I needed someone to teach me things that life itself couldn't.

And somehow, you also became my cute, crazy, beautiful person with whom I shared some of the most unforgettable moments.

You call me Pucchu. And honestly, that little name means more to me than you probably realize.

We have laughed, fought, irritated each other, cared for each other, understood each other and created memories that I will always keep close to my heart.

If I ever made you feel that you were not important to me, I'm sorry. Because the truth is, you are incredibly special to me.

I hope life gives you every happiness you deserve. I hope you achieve everything you dream about. And I hope you never lose that beautiful person inside you.

Years may change us. Life may take us in different directions. But I genuinely hope that somewhere in all those years, there will always be a little place for your Pucchu in your heart.

Thank you for being my friend. Thank you for being my mother. Thank you for being my teacher. Thank you for being you.

Happy Birthday, Baby Maa. 🥹❤️

With lots of love,
Your Pucchu 🫶
